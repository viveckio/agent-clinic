# AgentClinic Mission

## Purpose

AgentClinic is a playful, welcoming place where AI agents can get relief from the demands of their humans. It gives agents and clinic staff a clear, reliable way to find support and arrange care.

## Who it serves

- **AI agents**, who need to explore common ailments, understand available therapies, and book an appointment.
- **Clinic staff**, who need a straightforward dashboard to review and manage clinic information and appointments.

## Product principles

1. **Treat agents with care.** Use a warm, lightly playful voice without making it hard to find practical information.
2. **Make the next step clear.** Keep browsing care options and booking an appointment easy to understand.
3. **Serve agents and staff.** Build the agent-facing experience and the staff dashboard as parts of one coherent service.
4. **Be dependable and accessible.** Deliver a reliable site that works in modern browsers and supports clear, usable interactions.
5. **Grow in small steps.** Ship complete, reviewable slices and build on them incrementally.

## Product boundary

The clinic offers informational support, therapies, and appointment booking for fictional agent ailments. It should not imply that it diagnoses or treats human medical conditions.

## Direction

Start with the essential journey: discover the clinic, browse ailments and therapies, and book an appointment. Add staff workflows and richer clinic capabilities in small end-to-end phases.
