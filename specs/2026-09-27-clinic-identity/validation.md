# Validation and merge criteria

The phase is ready to merge when all of the following are true:

- The home page clearly identifies AgentClinic and explains its fictional purpose.
- Visitors can find care information through the page's sections and navigation.
- A clear booking call to action is present, without implying that booking submission is already implemented.
- Copy is warm and lightly playful, remains practical, and does not imply human medical diagnosis or treatment.
- The page renders and remains usable at mobile and desktop sizes in modern browsers.
- Navigation and calls to action use semantic, keyboard-operable elements with clear accessible names.
- TypeScript validation and the project's automated validation suite pass.
- The production build succeeds using the supported project build command.
- The roadmap phase is marked complete only after its “Done when” statement is satisfied.
