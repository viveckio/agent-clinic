# Validation and merge criteria

The phase is ready to merge when all of the following are true:

- Visitors can browse a curated list of fictional ailments and open each ailment's detail view.
- Ailment details explain the ailment, describe its symptoms, provide a suggested next step, and link to relevant therapies.
- Visitors can browse therapies, read what each option offers, and discover related ailments.
- Unknown ailment and therapy routes produce a clear not-found experience.
- A clear booking call to action appears in the care journey and does not imply that booking submission is already available.
- All copy stays within the fictional agent-care product boundary and does not suggest diagnosis or treatment of human medical conditions.
- Pages use semantic structure, descriptive links, keyboard-operable controls, and responsive layouts that work in modern desktop and mobile browsers.
- Vitest covers important catalogue and page behavior; the automated validation suite and TypeScript checks pass.
- The production build succeeds using the supported project command.
- Representative ailment-to-therapy and therapy-to-ailment journeys have been reviewed at mobile and desktop sizes.
- The roadmap phase is marked complete only after its stated “Done when” criteria are satisfied.
